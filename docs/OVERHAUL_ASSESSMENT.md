# F-22 overhaul assessment

Research and source review: **24 October 2026**. Reviewed baseline: [`ccec9ca147e15b4fadc13505495e4fee2e402752`](https://github.com/Ethanw05/DCS-F22/tree/ccec9ca147e15b4fadc13505495e4fee2e402752). Source links below are pinned to that revision.

## Decision

**Develop an independent F-22 aircraft around our existing EFM and assets, with new avionics and vehicle-system models.** Removing the F-15C requirement is a credible engineering objective. A feature-complete replacement for the inherited air-to-air radar/fire-control stack is **not yet demonstrated** with the interfaces available to this project. It must be the first integration experiment, alongside basic standalone startup.

The strongest immediate improvements are fuel/mass conservation, time-based actuators, physically consistent thrust vectoring, real ground-contact logic, shared system state, and a custom HUD. These are actionable without discovering unpublished F-22 parameters. A modern-looking display over inherited F-15 functions would leave the principal architectural limitation intact.

This assessment combines the [public resource catalogue](RESOURCES.md), targeted source tracing through registration, the EFM callbacks, active cockpit systems and indicators, and isolated arithmetic experiments. It is not a certification of every asset or every line. **No DCS runtime, Windows DLL build, multiplayer session or flight-performance validation was available for this review.** No aircraft behavior was changed by this documentation commit.

Implementation companions: [standalone design](STANDALONE_DESIGN.md) and [ordered roadmap with acceptance criteria](OVERHAUL_ROADMAP.md).

### Arithmetic verification performed

The unchanged baseline fuel functions were extracted into a temporary C++23 harness with their actual state declarations and utility types. This checks arithmetic/bookkeeping independently of DCS, not the host's mass handling. The harness was kept outside the repository; no audit tools were added.

| Experiment | Observed result |
| --- | --- |
| 8,199.8 kg internal, capacity 8,200 kg; refueling callback receives 0.6 kg | Quantity becomes **8,201.8 kg**; queued mass addition is only **0.2 kg**. |
| First external station initialized with 100 kg | Stored quantity becomes **1,850 kg**. |
| Burn 1.5 kg from external fuel | Aggregate becomes 1,848.5 kg; station remains 1,850 kg; no mass event is queued. |
| Initialize another station after that burn | Aggregate recomputes as 3,700 kg, restoring the first station's consumed amount. |
| Consumption requests 1.5 kg with only 0.1 kg internal and no external fuel | Actual quantity falls by 0.1 kg; queued mass loss is 1.5 kg. |

Separate evaluations of the active expressions show that a 20° nozzle command produces a force direction of **13.7047° at throttle output 0.67** and **21.8195° at 1.1**. The resulting force magnitude is 0.96723 and 1.01221 times the input thrust respectively. A pure rotation would preserve magnitude and commanded direction before any separately modeled loss.

The gear actuator takes 667 calls from 0 to 1: **2.001 s at dt=0.003, 4.002 s at 0.006, and 8.004 s at 0.012**. These are expression-level results; DCS's actual scheduling was not measured.

## HUD overhaul specification

The repo should gain a real HUD device and indicator, rather than continue dispatching inherited HUD controls. Start with an independently useful flight display: boresight, horizon/pitch ladder, velocity vector, airspeed, Mach, altitude, heading, vertical velocity, g, alpha and navigation/landing steering. Each symbol needs a source, a mode applicability rule, units, validity flag and clipping/caging behavior.

Keep a **symbol evidence sheet**: directly observed F-22 form; general display-engineering method; or our assumed presentation. Counter-pointer concepts from the public HUD research are useful, but avoid importing another aircraft's entire layout. Treat the historical rig footage and the 2002 illustration as different evidence contexts. Do not infer all A/A or A/G modes from either.

Flight-path symbology must use an explicitly defined inertial/air-relative velocity convention and correct body-to-view transformations, with no arbitrary screen-space offsets substituted for angular projection. Validate boresight alignment and collimation with head movement in 2D and both VR eyes. Handle invalid air data, excessive off-screen displacement and unusual attitudes visibly. Combat cues require valid tracks and weapon solutions; a drawn target box or range scale does not establish radar guidance.

## Recommended overhaul boundary

Retain licensed models, textures, sounds, useful input bindings and display primitives. Refactor the EFM behind its existing DCS callback boundary, replacing questionable mathematics and state ownership in stages. Rebuild mission avionics, HUD, navigation, stores logic and vehicle systems around explicit interfaces. Maintain separate AI definitions and player implementation while keeping their high-level configuration consistent.

Use a conventional production F-22A profile as the first fidelity target, with individually documented 3.2B-era capabilities where supported. Treat this as an engineering default, not a claim to have recovered an exact operational software tape. Experimental weapons, the custom IRST pod and later upgrades belong in named optional profiles. **Authenticity should mean traceable public evidence plus disclosed approximations, not unsupported precision.**
