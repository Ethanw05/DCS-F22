# Chinese forum and community research — follow-up

Date: **24 October 2026**. Scope: public Chinese-language discussion, Q&A, aviation communities and their citation trails. This follows the [first Chinese-language pass](CHINESE_RESEARCH.md). Research/documentation only; no aircraft implementation changes.

## Result and how to use these leads

The useful outcome is a set of source trails, including improved access to [R65](RESOURCES.md#r65), a test-pilot talk/interview trail [R173–R174](RESOURCES.md#r173), and architecture/testing references [R175–R176](RESOURCES.md#r175). The [audit addendum](audit/README.md#retired-report-a19) maps these to the systems they can inform.

Forum posts are retained below as **discovery leads**, with their actual access status. They are not automatically promoted to aircraft-accuracy references. An author's expertise in another aircraft, a convincing chart, a military-looking document title or a large comment count does not establish production F-22 data. Reposts of one source remain one source.

No authenticated F-22 flight manual, complete operational HUD symbol book or new calibrated production performance database was verified in this follow-up. This does not claim that every archive or unindexed attachment has been searched.

## Source trails worth continuing

### 1. Chinese YF-22 scan → original Barham paper

[Chinese scan, page 1](https://doc.xuehai.net/b7d77cad47c192110174671ca.html) and [page 2](https://doc.xuehai.net/b7d77cad47c192110174671ca-2.html) reproduce a March 1996 article under a translation title. OCR is poor and translator/bibliographic details remain incomplete. The matching original is [R65](RESOURCES.md#r65); selected original-paper text is now accessible through a public mirror. Use the original, with prototype provenance preserved.

### 2. PTT Brown translation → original talk and interview

CF01 points to the [2022 presentation](RESOURCES.md#r173). A separate [2026 first-person interview/transcript](RESOURCES.md#r174) was retrieved. Selected passages support qualitative systems/handling study; presentation acceleration claims still require original slide/audio verification, aircraft mass and conditions. Do not convert recollected observations into universal limits.

### 3. Bilibili bibliography → specific journal titles

CF06 names two studies, with spelling variants corrected during publisher searches:

- *喷口修形对二元喷管红外特征影响的数值研究*: [publisher record](https://stae.com.cn/jsygc/article/html/1409097?st=article_issue). Publisher-indexed abstract located; direct retrieval failed. Generic numerical nozzle study; no F119 fit established.
- *不同喷口修形的二元收敛喷管RCS数值模拟*: bibliographic trail located in [another journal paper](https://www.jasp.com.cn/hkdlxb/cn/article/doi/10.13224/j.cnki.jasp.2014.11.011). Exact original record/full text not yet recovered. Do not treat either its figures or CF06's interpretation as actual F-22 signature measurements.

The associated avionics article is named in Bilibili's series cards, but its exact canonical article URL was not verified. It remains a follow-up lead, not a reviewed source. Architecture searches also reached [R175–R176](RESOURCES.md#r175) through journal bibliography chains.
